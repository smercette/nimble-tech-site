The Nimble Pass

One wallet. Every Nimble tool.

The Nimble Pass is a single set of credits that works across every product we build. Top up once, then use the same balance to bundle a court hearing in BundleRight today, and whatever we ship next week.

Buy credits when you need them. No subscription, no monthly fee, no annual lock-in. We're finalising per-credit pricing ahead of public launch. Credits stay valid for 12 months from your most recent top-up — keep using us and your balance keeps rolling.

Spend them anywhere in the family. Today that means BundleRight. Tomorrow it'll mean more — and your credits will already work there.
