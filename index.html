Security

The same answers, whoever asks.

This is the part of a bespoke build that normally costs you a six-week risk assessment. Because your tool runs on the shared Nimble platform, the answers below are already settled and already in use.

AI through AWS Bedrock
Models are called through AWS Bedrock with no retention, no prompt logging and no training on what you send. Your drafting does not become anyone's dataset.
Encrypted in transit and at rest
TLS 1.3 to the application, encrypted storage behind it. No unencrypted copy of your documents exists at any point.
UK infrastructure, private routing
Hosted in AWS eu-west-2. The AI endpoint is reached over a private network path inside our own infrastructure, with no route out to the public internet.
Matter labels we cannot read
Your readable matter names are encrypted with a key your firm holds. We store an opaque code and an opaque ciphertext, and we hold no key to either.
Minimal data model
We keep what the tool needs to do its job and to bill you correctly. We do not collect client data for any other purpose, because we have no other purpose for it.
Deletion on request
Ask us to remove your firm's data and we remove it, to a defined timetable written into the agreement rather than left to goodwill.
In practice

What a bespoke tool actually looks like.

A worked example of the shape. This is the kind of job that earns a bespoke build, rather than a tool you could pick off a shelf.

The job. A commercial team held several hundred client contracts across shared drives and old matter files. The break dates, notice windows and auto-renewal clauses sat inside the documents and nowhere else. Missing a notice window meant a client tied in for another year, and the first anyone knew of it was the renewal invoice.

The tool. Drop in a folder of contracts. Each one comes back with its term, renewal basis, notice window and break date pulled out and cited to the clause it came from, and the whole set lands as a dated register the team can actually work from.

Why it had to be bespoke. Nothing off the shelf knew the firm's own templates or the way it records key dates. The extraction had to follow their house definitions, and the register had to drop straight into the diary system they already use.
